The Strategic Read
This is the part worth thinking about before the page count

Your pipeline is roofers. A competitor that already sells roofing is sitting on both halves of that transaction — they can approach the same roofing customers directly, and they can approach your roofers with a better offer than a referral fee.

You cannot stop that. What you can do is stop being invisible, so that a roofer forwarding your name has something to forward, and so that some jobs reach you without a referrer in the middle.

And the encouraging half
  • Hub City Solar publishes three pages. Three. The bar in this market is not high.
  • Neither firm is running the kind of advertising that would be hard to catch up with — we found no Meta ads attributable to either in our search.
  • You are a specialist. Pure Energy sells four things; you install solar. For a homeowner who wants the job done right, that is a stronger story, and nobody has ever heard it because there is nowhere to tell it.
  • You have capacity now. Five to ten more installs is a real answer to "how soon can you start", and most firms cannot say it.
What The Lubbock Market Has Going For It
Worth saying on a page, once you have one
  • The sun is the easy part. West Texas is among the better solar resources in the country, and homeowners here broadly know it.
  • The hard part is trust — solar has a reputation problem built by door-knockers and national finance companies. A local installer with a real address, real photographs and named customers wins on exactly the axis the industry is weakest on.
  • You are already the referral choice of roofers, which is a trust signal most companies would pay a lot for and cannot buy.
What We Could Not Check
Marked honestly rather than guessed
  • Pure Energy's homepage is JavaScript-rendered, so its forms, phone links and any on-page reviews were not readable by our check and have not been assessed.
  • Anyone's reviews, ratings, pricing, revenue, install volume, crew size or finance terms, including yours.
  • Whether any of these firms advertise on Google, Meta or locally. Our Meta Ad Library search returned nothing attributable to any of them, which is not proof that none exists.
  • Your own Facebook page — it exists but Facebook required a login, so nothing on it has been read.
  • Every solar installer in Lubbock. This is the set we could find and verify, not a complete census, and it excludes the national firms that sell into this market.

The best-known firm in town publishes three pages. You publish none.

This is not a market you have to out-spend. It is one you have to turn up in.
